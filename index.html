The Philosophy

When the tide pulls back, space opens

The ocean doesn't apologise for its tides. Low tide isn't a failure — it's a reveal. When the water recedes, what was hidden becomes visible: the texture of the seabed, the quiet life beneath the surface.


Slowing down works the same way. Not as giving up, but as creating the conditions for something real to emerge. Low Tide is a practice of intentional deceleration — stepping out of the noise of wellness culture that demands constant optimisation, and returning instead to the body, the breath, and the present moment.


This is not a place to achieve. It is a place to arrive.

"The antidote to overwhelm is not doing more — it's learning to be."

What We Practise

Four doorways back to yourself

Each session weaves together one or more of these practices — always gently, always at your pace.

○

Breathwork

Guided breath practices drawn from somatic and nervous system traditions. Accessible for all levels — no experience needed, just a willingness to breathe.

∿

Nervous System Regulation

Practices rooted in polyvagal theory that help your body find its way back to a felt sense of safety — gently, without force.

◌

Meditation

Soft, open-awareness meditation. Not about emptying the mind, but learning to rest with what's here — thoughts, sensations, and all.

≈

Slow Movement

Gentle, embodied movement — part somatic yoga, part intuitive flow. Designed to help you reconnect with your body, not perform for it.
